# 🏦 BankPulse-AI

## Enterprise Banking Retention Intelligence Platform

BankPulse-AI is an AI-powered customer churn analytics system designed for banking institutions.  
The platform combines Machine Learning, Business Intelligence, Explainable AI, and Executive Analytics Dashboards to identify high-risk customers and optimize customer retention strategies.

# 📌 Business Problem

Customer churn is one of the largest revenue risks in the banking industry.

This project predicts whether a customer is likely to churn and provides:

- risk segmentation,
- retention recommendations,
- business impact analysis,
- customer intelligence insights.

---

# 💰 Business Cost Optimization

The project prioritizes minimizing business loss instead of only maximizing accuracy.

| Error Type | Business Impact |
|---|---|
| False Negative | ₹40,000 Loss |
| False Positive | ₹500 Cost |

The ML pipeline is optimized for:
- High Recall
- Reduced False Negatives
- Maximum Retention Intelligence

# 📈 Key Business Insights

- High complaint frequency strongly correlates with churn.
- Digitally disengaged customers show elevated churn probability.
- Lower product utilization increases churn risk.
- AI-driven retention strategies can significantly reduce business loss.
